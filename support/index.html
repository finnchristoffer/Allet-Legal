Support

We’re here to help.

Need help with Allet, Allet+, your account, or your data? Start here.

Subscriptions and purchases

To restore an Allet+ purchase, open Allet’s paywall and choose Restore purchases. To manage or cancel an Apple subscription, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad, then select Allet.

Data and privacy requests

You can request help with access, correction, export, or deletion of your Allet data. Email support.allet@gmail.com with a clear subject such as “Allet data deletion request”.

Contact

Email support.allet@gmail.com. We aim to respond within five business days. Allet is operated by Finn Christoffer Kurniawan and Sebastian Putra, individuals based in Indonesia.

Before you contact us

  • For a purchase issue, include the App Store receipt or the approximate purchase date. Do not send payment-card details.
  • For a bug, include your app version, iPhone model, and a short description of what happened. Do not send screenshots containing sensitive financial details unless requested through a secure support process.
