Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Allet is an expense companion. This policy explains how Allet handles information when you use the app, Allet+ subscriptions, and this website.

1. Information Allet handles

Information you create in Allet

  • Transaction details, such as merchant name, amount, date, category, and notes.
  • Account information when you choose to upgrade from an anonymous account, including an email address or identity-provider details.
  • Subscription status and purchase identifiers needed to provide Allet+.

Receipt and voice features

  • Receipt images are processed on your device and are not uploaded by Allet. OCR text extracted from a receipt may be sent to Allet’s processing services to categorize the transaction.
  • When you use voice input, microphone audio may be processed by Apple’s speech-recognition service or sent to Google Gemini, depending on the feature mode you choose. Allet does not intentionally store your audio recording after processing.
  • OCR text, voice transcripts, and AI-generated categorization output may be processed by Google Gemini and may be included in Allet’s AI-observability events in PostHog while the service operates.

Technical and usage information

  • Device, app-version, diagnostic, crash, and usage information.
  • An app or account identifier used to operate the service and prevent abuse.

2. Why we use information

We use information to provide and secure Allet, save and synchronize your transactions, categorize receipts and voice entries, manage subscriptions, troubleshoot failures, understand feature use, and respond to support or data requests.

3. Service providers and international processing

Allet uses service providers that process information on our behalf:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, and backend services. Allet’s production data is hosted in Singapore.
  • Google Gemini to process OCR text, voice transcripts, and voice recordings used with AI categorization features.
  • Apple for server-side speech recognition when that voice mode is used.
  • RevenueCat and Apple to provide and verify Allet+ purchases.
  • PostHog for product analytics and AI observability.
  • Sentry for crash and error diagnostics.

These providers may process data outside your country. We use them only to operate, maintain, and improve the services described in this policy.

4. Retention

We retain account and transaction information while your account is active or as needed to provide Allet. Diagnostic and analytics data is retained according to the relevant provider’s configured retention period. We may retain limited information where necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting purposes.

5. Your choices and requests

You can manage subscriptions through your Apple Account settings. You may request access, correction, export, or deletion of your Allet data through the support channel below. We may need to verify that a request is connected to your account before acting on it.

For access, correction, export, or deletion requests, email support.allet@gmail.com with the subject “Allet data request”. We may need to verify that a request is connected to your account before acting on it.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Allet or our data practices change. We will post the updated version here and revise the “Last updated” date.
